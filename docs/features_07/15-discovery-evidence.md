# Phase 15 qualification

Implemented on 2026-10-10 in the `p15-discovery` workspace on main `a7d24a97`,
with phases 14 and 17 landed.
[Decision 0057](../decisions/0057-discovery.md) records the port, the
derivations, the record layout and the crate. No session message, identity
or secret-store format changed; the starter's options and their Kotlin and
native shapes did. [Discovery](../runtime/discovery.md) is the guide.

## Slice 1 checks

- `iroh-dns` 1.3.0 signs a packet under any key; `PkarrRelayClient` publishes
  and resolves it by that key; `iroh-dns-server` 1.3.0 stores any packet whose
  signature matches the key in its path. Read from the three sources. Nothing
  was published to a public service.
- `iroh-mdns-address-lookup` 0.6.0 depends on iroh 1.x, takes a service name
  and builds for `aarch64-apple-ios`, `aarch64-apple-ios-sim`,
  `aarch64-linux-android` and `x86_64-linux-android`
  (`./sdks/native/check-ios`, the Android runtime build).

Neither failed, so no replacement is named.

Kotlin: `PeerTransport.Iroh` and `RockyEndpoint.bind` take `lookup`, `topic`
and `local`; `discovery()`, `NetworkStatus.discovery`, `Readiness.discovery`
and `Peer.isLocal` report the paths. The UniFFI ABI is unchanged, since the
options travel as JSON; both bindings were regenerated by the builds below.
`DiscoveryTest` (JVM) covers the status, a TCP transport, the lock holder's
logic across background and foreground, and a member finding its issuer over
mDNS. `sdks/kotlin/android-discovery-check` is the app the emulator gate
installs and removes.

The relay run found a defect that loopback never showed: a borrowed endpoint
dialed on the database's runtime, so its connections' drivers ended with the
database and the endpoint then waited for them forever at close. The
attachment now dials on the runtime it was made on; the run asserts that both
endpoints close within 5 s.
